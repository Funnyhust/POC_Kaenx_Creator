### 1 Introduction
This volume of the KNX Specifications describes the KNX Association’s Certification Scheme for
products.

This volume of the KNX Specifications is divided in the following clauses:

- Clause 1: Introduction

- Clause 2: Definitions

- Clause 3: Various phases of the KNX Association Certification Scheme for products (introductory
phase, registration, testing, certification, surveillance, re-certification and reassessment as well as a
number of special cases)

- Clause 4: Overview of the various forms needed for registration and testing

- Clause 5: Description of a number of special KNX certification related aspects such as Application
Notes, etc.

- Clause 6: Presentation of the tools used during the KNX Association Certification Scheme for
Products (support for creation of a product entry by means of the Manufacturer Tool (MT) etc.)
This Volume does NOT contain the actual conformance (test) requirements: it merely makes reference to
them (see Table 4).

### 3 Description of the KNX Certification Process

#### 3.1 Scope of the KNX Certification Scheme for Products
In line with the KNX Association’s statutes, the purpose of the KNX Association’s Certification scheme
for products is the following.

- Achieve a defined minimum degree of compatibility between products of different vendors (i.e.
member companies of KNX Association).

- Establish the KNX trademark on a worldwide level as a token for quality.

- Increase the market success of the KNX technology in Home and Building Systems Engineering.

Owing to this Certification Scheme, the KNX logo signifies that:
When used in connection with an application product:

− The product complies with a product standard as chosen by the applicant as well as any (additional
and/or higher) requirements of [03].
Devices, exclusively based on IP, which are tested according to the hardware requirements given
in [03] can bear the KNX trademark.
Devices, exclusively based on IP, which are not tested according to the hardware requirements
given in [03] cannot bear the KNX trademark[1]. Products, accessing KNX installations through a
KNX interface, cannot be branded with the KNX Trademark or any of the existing labels.

− The product complies with the minimum KNX system requirements for application products (see

[06]), including a defined minimum degree of interworking according to [07] and functionality
according to the manufacturer’s specifications.

When used in connection with KNX standardised basic and system components:

  - The product complies (if applicable) with the minimum KNX system requirements for basic and
system components (see [06] respectively [11]).
  - The product complies with the KNX hardware requirements as laid down in [03].

− The product complies with the entire set of hardware interface requirements as laid down in [11]
(including the features marked as ‘standardised’).
When used in connection with KNX non-standardised basic and system components

− They comply with the requirements as stated in [11] of the KNX Specifications but merely to those
marked as mandatory (specific requirements to ensure that bus communication is not impaired).

1 KNX application running on such platforms may however still be submitted to KNX certification, i.e. checked
only against [06] and [07] and not against [03].

##### 3.2.2 In-depth Study of involved parties in KNX Certification

The following parties are involved in the KNX Association’s Certification Scheme for products:

1. The applicant, i.e. a KNX Association member company.
2. The KNX Association (proprietor of the KNX Trademark) respectively its Certification Department
acting as a registration and certification body.
  - The operation of this department is run in accordance with the requirements of ISO 17065
(General criteria for bodies operating product certification). The KNX Association’s Certification
Department is not nationally accredited under ISO 17065. Appeals regarding KNX certification of
products have to be addressed to the KNX Management Board.
  - One of the consequences of applying this European standard is the full neutrality and integrity
guaranteed by the KNX certification Schemes. Any expert to whom KNX Association takes
recourse in the framework of the operation of the KNX Association’s Certification Scheme
(including the members of the Management Board in case of an appeal) is obliged to sign a
confidentiality agreement.
  - During assessment of the operation of testing laboratories, the KNX Association’s certification
department together with its designated experts takes into account the requirements of
ISO/IEC 17011 ‘General requirements for accreditation bodies accrediting conformity assessment
bodies’.
3. The KNX Association’s Certification steering bodies

  - The Certification Working group (KonCert) and its task forces, responsible for the drafting,
extension and maintenance of the KNX Association’s Certification procedure for products
including test specifications and test tools. Both representatives of KNX shareholders as well as
representatives from the KNX accredited Test Labs can apply to become permanent or
corresponding members of the KNX Certification Working Group.
  - The KNX Management Board is the ultimate body of appeal, responsible for the surveillance of
the Certification process and the continued application of the ISO 17065 as the basis for the work
in the KNX Association’s Certification Department.
The third party KNX accredited Test Labs[ 2)], of which the tasks are outlined in the underneath clauses.

2) For a description of the KNX Association’s accreditation procedure for test labs, refer to clause 5.8

##### 3.4.1 Introductory Phase

1. If a company has developed a KNX compatible product and wants to brand it with the KNX logo, it
shall address KNX Association to inquire about the conditions for KNX membership (if not yet a
member). The majority of the KNX Association’s Management Board shall approve the application
for membership in case the candidate applies for shareholdership, else the team of KNX directors.

**NOTE 1: Should the company decide to renounce its membership in KNX Association, according to the stipulations of the**
Trademark License Agreement it also renounces its right to use the KNX logo, even if its registered and/or certified products
(continue to) comply with the KNX requirements
2. As a second step, the new member shall purchase a copy of the KNX Manufacturer Tool, in order to
be able to generate the ETS product data entries required for certification of products[3].Until further
notice, such a KNX product data entry is waived for E-Mode compatible products. However, after a
maximum of 6 months after the availability of the ETS extensions able to handle E-Mode, the
applicant is obliged to deliver the required database entry. If he fails to do so, KNX Association has
the right to withdraw an already granted certificate.
3. As a third step the KNX member shall request in its MyKNX account the manufacturer code for its

#### 3.5 Registration types

The MyKNX Product registration tool allows the main/OEM customer to select a number of pre-defined
registration types after the knxei file has been uploaded. Depending on the selected registration type,
different information has to be entered/filled out.

##### 3.5.1 New

###### 3.5.1.1 New product
This relates to a

- new hardware (with unique hardware key) with a corresponding
- new product, containing a new commercial order number

created in the Manufacturer Tool. Both hardware and product were not yet part of the product catalog of
the KNX manufacturer.

The KNX main manufacturer has to confirm when submitting a product for registration in MyKNX upon
completing the hardware datasheet that the hardware complies with at least the standards as specified in
the Figures 1 and 2 in the Volume 4/1 of the KNX Specifications.

###### 3.5.1.2 New Application Program
This registration type concerns a new Application Program with a unique key that has not been registered
before by KNX Association. This registration type in all cases imply software tests that have to be carried
out by a KNX accredited Test Lab.

###### 3.5.1.3 Product Group
Products with modifications solely related to colour and/or markings may be combined in a product
group: if desired by the manufacturer the order numbers may be combined to one number containing wild
cards (e.g. ‘*’ or ‘?’). KNX Association will attribute one KNX registration number to the entire product
group in combination with one Application Program.

**Products with modifications exceeding the above are considered as different products and will receive**
in combination with an Application Program different KNX registration numbers. These products may
not be combined to product groups, neither may they be grouped under one order number with wild card.

NOTE 3 In the KNX Manufacturer Tool, only products belonging to a same product group may be defined as additional catalogue
entries belonging to the same ETS product, as shown in clause 6.1.2 of this Volume.

##### 3.5.2 Reassessment

###### 3.5.2.1 Introduction
The following rules apply for products that emanate from the same manufacturer and are modified as
regards hardware and software during the validity of the certificate (so called reassessment procedure).

###### 3.5.2.2 Reassessment product
In this case, the registered/certified product has undergone some changes. Based on the declared changes,
KNX Association will assess whether it is a Reassessment or New application and whether tests are
required or not.

If the product to be registered has the same hardware platform, but:

a) Contains less channels/outputs than the master product;
b) Has a different amperage/voltage than the master product
c) Has less sensor functionality than the master product

then, in this case, the application can be considered as a “Reassessment product”. If “reassessment
product” is chosen, then a reference shall be made to the original product.


-----

If the product to be registered has a different hardware platform, then the product will be considered as a
“New” product.


-----

###### 3.5.2.3 Reassessment Application Program
In this case, the registered/certified Application Program has undergone some changes. Based on the
declared changes, KNX Association will assess on a case-by-case basis, whether it is really a
reassessment or a New application and whether tests are required or not. If “reassessment Application
Program” is chosen, then a reference shall be made to the original Application program.
Certification relevant modifications especially relate to the product’s bus and/or download behaviour, e.g.
modification of Group Objects, parameters, supported client/server management services, etc.
Modifications to the ETS plug-in user interface are in principle not certification relevant.

The following rules are applicable:

3.5.2.3.1 Binary Code changed  New:
Applied software modifications involving change of the binary code or extension of the number of
parameters/objects in all cases imply a renewed registration and certification.

3.5.2.3.2 Binary Code unchanged and there are:
a) Fewer parameters / fewer group objects
b) Only new parameters added
c) New parameters that address other locations in an already registered binary file

Then, in this case, the application can be considered as a “Reassessment Application Program”.

**a) Fewer parameters / fewer group objects**

In case the Binary Code is unchanged and the Application Program contains fewer parameters and group
objects, then in this case there are no tests required at all, provided that the master/original application
program has been tested successfully.

**b) Only new parameters added**

When new parameters (i.e. no dummy parameters) are added to an already certified binary image-file, this
will always lead to renewed tests and certification. However, in this case, conformity tests can be reduced
to the full testing of the extended functionality and a random test of the unchanged part of the application.

**c) New parameters that address other locations in an already registered binary file**

In this case, a complete re-test of the Application Program is required.

##### 3.5.3 Recombination

###### 3.5.3.1 Recombination of Parts already certified in other Combinations
A certified KNX product consists of the following parts: a BAU (whether or not integrated), an
Application Program and an application module.

If parts are recombined, which have once shown compliance to the KNX requirements (be it system,
interworking or hardware requirements) in any combination of above-mentioned parts, it lies in the
responsibility of the manufacturer to check the correct functioning of this new combination.

Although an application at KNX Association for this new combination is compulsory (via the MyKNX
product registration tool), tests at a KNX accredited Test Lab is not. However, in the framework of
market surveillance KNX Association still reserves the right to oblige re-tests of this new combination at
a KNX accredited Test Lab.

###### 3.5.3.2 Combination of Parts already certified in other Combinations with new Parts
Exchanging a part of a certified product by a new uncertified part (be it a new BAU, a modified user
program or a modified hardware) results in the checking of compliance of this new part only (according
to the valid KNX Specifications requirements). Is this new part a

- Hardware, then a renewed knxei file shall be submitted in which the hardware datasheet has to be
completed;

- Software, then a renewed knxei file shall be submitted for which the software datasheet has to be
completed and the relevant KNX system conformance test report shall be established by a KNX
accredited Test Lab.

##### 3.5.4 OEM Application

###### 3.5.4.1 OEM Product / Application program
A derived product or an OEM product is a product that has been developed and manufactured by a KNX
member company and sold on to another KNX member company who then relabels this product with its
own company logo and applies for KNX certification for this product.

Products can only be considered as ‘derived/OEM products’ if the extend of the modifications do not
**exceed**

- Colour

- Marking

- Modified housing

- Position of terminals not affecting electrical safety

- Position of operational elements and indicators

- Changed fixing means

- Original certified software, modifications only related to reduced number of parameters,
manufacturer code, device type, version number and commercial data.
If they do, they are considered as new products for which an application for a “New” product shall be
submitted to KNX Association by means of the MyKNX product registration tool.

For both OEM product and Application Program, the OEM manufacturer has to refer to the original
product and application program of the Main manufacturer.

Referring to the original product and original Application Program is only possible if the Main
Manufacturer has released the corresponding Main Product and Application Program for OEM
deployment to his OEM customer.

#### 4.4 PICS and PIXIT 

##### 4.4.1 KNX Protocol Implementation Conformance Statement (PICS)

The KNX PICS proforma allow the applicant to state compliance to the mandatory features of one of the
standardised profiles of [06] as well as information on optional implemented features of one of the
standardised profiles of [06].

This PICS proforma shall be provided for any product, of which the system software stack is either
uncertified or partly certified (for definitions of certified/uncertified system stack, see [09]). Further
information on the implementation of the supported KNX services shall be provided in the PICS and
PIXIT proforma for implemented KNX Services. Form can be requested directly by contacting KNX
Association.

##### 4.4.2 PIXIT
The following different types of PIXIT Forms currently exist:

1. A form for providing additional information on the implementation of the KNX services (PICS and
PIXIT for KNX Services). For use, see clause 4.4.1
2. A ‘KNX PIXIT Header’ form for providing an overview of the Datapoints implemented in a given
application as well as an identification/functional description of the to be tested application.
3. A form for describing the individual implemented Datapoints in a given application, i.e. the KNX
Datapoint description form
4. A form for describing the BDUT’s (Bus Device Under Test) behaviour on

  - Initialisation.
  - Failure and return of bus power.
  - (if applicable) Failure and return of ancillary power.
A KNX accredited Test Lab has the right to accept forms of presentation which are different from the
PIXIT proforma standardised by KNX Association, provided the contents of these documents is equal to
or exceeds the information normally provided by PIXIT proforma.
The applicant therefore has the following three options[ 11)].
1. Either he elaborates the PIXIT proforma using the word templates available from KNX Association
as a starting point;
2. Or he makes sure that the internal software documentation he submits to the Test Lab contains at least
the same information as the one he would normally submit by means of the PIXIT proforma.
3. Or last but not least, a combination of the two cases above, e.g. where the applicant uses the word
templates available from KNX Association but makes explicit reference to existing software
documentation, which he/she attaches to the filled out KNX forms.
The PIXIT proforma may be rationalised, if a certain Datapoint description applies to a number of objects
or parameters. It is in this case not strictly necessary to fill in one Datapoint description per
object/parameter.
The PIXIT proforma shall at least be signed on the PIXIT header form.

#### 4.5 Company Type Test Reports
Company type test reports shall be compliant to the relevant EN/ISO/IEC 17025 requirements.

Restricting test results simply to ‘pass’ or ‘fail’ is only acceptable if the standard, on which a test is based,
does not strictly oblige the tests body to document the values during the test and if a result can only be
documented by means of ‘pass’ or ‘fail’.

#### 5.4 Transitional Period between new Issues of Requirements
The transitional period between two versions of the KNX Specifications is fixed to 1 year (if not decided
otherwise by the KTB). The transitional period for Application Notes is specified explicitly in the heading
of the Application Notes.

#### 5.5 Tests of Products on the Basis of obsolete Requirements
Generally, tests of products (especially system and basic components/devices) shall always be carried out
on the basis of the last valid edition of the KNX Specifications. There are however two exceptions.

1. If the manufacturer has placed an order for the execution of conformance tests (to be carried out on
the basis of a preceding version of the KNX requirements) at a KNX accredited Test Lab at the latest
four weeks prior to the elapsing of the transitional period of a new issue of the KNX Specifications or
Application Note.
2. If the manufacturer has applied at KTB for an exceptional permission to be able to have his product
tested on the basis of an obsolete version of the KNX Specifications. A manufacturer shall also apply
for such an exceptional permission if a given Application Note has come into effect (after the
transitional period has elapsed) that had not been taken into account at the time of the development of
a given product.

It is not allowed to certify products on a mixture of requirements of different versions of the KNX
Specifications, e.g. partly on the KNX Specifications version 1.0 and partly on version 1.1 or 2.0.

#### 5.6 Use of KNX Logo on unregistered Products
The use of the KNX logo on unregistered products is principally not allowed.

For a start, any company that wants to label its products with the KNX logo shall have signed the KNX
Trademark and IPR License agreement.

If these agreements have been signed, the company may brand unregistered products with the KNX logo
for publicity reasons (in product catalogues, brochures, on fairs, etc.) but ONLY if KNX Association has
been informally notified by the manufacturer: informal notification here means that registration is not
[required, a simple email to certification@knx.org suffices.](mailto:certification@knx.org)

However, a company may never sell unregistered products branded with the KNX logo until registration
of the respective product has been filed at KNX Association.

Registration in this context means, submitting it by means of the MyKNX Product Registration Tool.
Only after KNX Association has registered the product, the product may be labelled with the KNX logo
and be commercialised. KNX Association has the right to withdraw any previously granted registration.

The six months’ time delay of the Registration Procedure (after which the registered product shall be duly
tested and certified) starts right after the registration of the corresponding product/Application Program
by KNX Association.

#### 5.7 Elaboration of CE declarations for KNX devices

A CE declaration shall cover all directives relevant for the product, in particular the Low Voltage (LV)directive and EMC directive. It shall be established according to the requirements as given in [03].

#### 5.8 KNX Association’s Accreditation Procedure for Test Labs

##### 5.8.1 General

Interested and qualified third party tests institutions may enter into a bilateral co-operation agreement
with KNX Association. As a prerequisite, such parties shall meet the KNX Requirements for the
**Accreditation of Tests Institutions.**

For this, they shall pass the resulting assessment by KNX Association, the extend of which may, in the
case of national accreditation, be restricted to ascertaining the capability to assess products to the KNX
requirements and to run the KNX tests as described in [08] of these KNX Specifications.

For each type of test (Interworking and Functionality tests, Layer test, etc.) a separate application for
accreditation is necessary. Accreditation for a specific type of test is only possible, after KNX Association
has officially published the respective test specifications. Candidate KNX accredited test labs shall first
succeed in achieving accreditation for Interworking and Functionality tests, before an application for
achieving accreditation for lower layer and security testing can be submitted.

In case the Test Lab is not nationally accredited according EN/ISO/IEC17025, it shall submit a copy of an
ISO 9001 certificate of an accredited certification body for the testing operation.

For the execution of assessment rounds at applying Test Labs, KNX Association has set up an assessment
team, consisting of a trained auditor (from the KNX Association’s staff members) and external (technical)
assessors, who have been recruited from KNX Association’s member companies and have duly signed a
confidentiality declaration.

Representatives of KNX accredited Test Labs are entitled to request participation in the KNX
Certification Group as a permanent or corresponding member (see clause 3.2 of this volume).

##### 5.8.2 Procedure
As a preliminary phase and in order to acquire the necessary experience in the field of KNX, any
interested Test Lab may submit a letter of intent (text available from KNX Association’s Certification
Department). This letter of intent allows the Test Lab to purchase any material available at KNX
Association (e.g. conformance test tools) at membership prices. This preliminary phase is limited to one
year. Once this year has elapsed, the Test Lab is obliged to submit a formal application for KNX
accreditation.

Any interested Test Lab shall submit a formal application by means of the forms available at the KNX
Certification Department.

If the department, in which the to be set-up KNX Test Lab is located, has been nationally accredited
according EN/ISO/IEC 17025, a copy of the acquired certificate shall be sent along with the filled out
application forms.

If the department, in which the to be set-up KNX Test Lab is located, has not been nationally accredited
according EN/ISO/IEC 17025, a copy of an acquired ISO 9001 certificate covering its testing operation
shall be sent along with the filled out application forms.

Every KNX test engineer working at the KNX candidate test lab shall at least hold a KNX certificate
proving that he/she successfully attended a KNX basic course (the number of the MyKNX account
including the acquired certificate shall be communicated to KNX when applying or when a new KNX test
engineer is hired).

Every KNX test engineer working at a KNX test lab wishing to achieve accreditation for lower layer or
security, shall hold a KNX tutor certificate[14].

14 For existing test labs, a transitional period is fixed to supply any missing certificates, i.e. until end of 2019.


-----

The applying Test Lab shall subsequently select a KNX device and corresponding Application Program
from a manufacturer of its choice, on the basis of which it can carry out a sample test. This device test
shall check the conformance of the device as regards Interworking/Functionality for a new candidate test
lab, for lower layer or security testing for an existing test lab wishing to extend its accreditation scope.
The device test shall be carried out on the basis of the KNX requirements as documented in the KNX
Specifications: for this, the Test Lab will have to require the necessary input (PIXIT, etc.) from the
manufacturer of the selected device.

Application for accreditation for KNX conformance tests is only possible, if KNX Association has duly
published the respective test specifications as an Approved Standard. It shall be taken into account that
for an application for lower layer testing and/or security, the candidate Test Lab needs to require from a
manufacturer of his choice not only a device with the implemented stack but possibly also sample
applications for running some of the Lower Layer tests.

The test results shall be documented in a sample test report on the basis of the KNX standardised test
report template, a copy of which is available from the KNX Association. Use of this standardised KNX
test report template is mandatory; Test Lab specific cover sheets may be added.

This sample test report shall be submitted to KNX Association. In the framework of a workshop of the
assessment team, this report will be evaluated and any first comments will be passed on to the applicant.

If the outcome of the above workshop is positive, KNX Association will then take the necessary steps for
the execution of an assessment round at the applicant’s premises, which will consist of proficiency tests
using the submitted sample test report as a reference, in order to assess the KNX aptitude of the Test Lab
staff members. Proficiency of the Test Lab engineers carrying out the relevant KNX tests is required,
proficiency of the head of the Test Lab only is not sufficient to achieve KNX accreditation.

The assessment team will be limited to 1 staff member from KNX Association and one external expert

The delegation of the KNX candidate test lab shall consist of at maximum the KNX test engineer(s), the
Head of the KNX test lab and optionally the Director of the candidate test lab (or his representative). The
assessment duration depends on the number of tests, for which a Test Lab has filed for accreditation (e.g.
if only for interworking and functionality tests, limited to one day).

Only the costs for the staff members from KNX Association have to be borne by the applicant. KNX
Association will draw from its own financial resources to refund the travelling expenses, living
allowances and any other required fees of the selected external assessment team member.

If the outcome of the audit is positive, KNX Association will issue the KNX accreditation certificate and
invoice the incurred costs (Scale of fees can be obtained from the KNX Association’s Certification
Department).

If the outcome is negative, the laid down measures shall be corrected within the time specified by the
assessment team: if this is not possible, the entire assessment round has to be repeated.

If the Test Lab was nationally accredited, the validity of the KNX certificate is identical to the national
EN/ISO/IEC 17025 certificate. Every three years the latter type of KNX accredited Test Lab is submitted
to a surveillance audit.

If the Test Lab was not nationally accredited, the validity of the certificate is identical to that of the ISO
9001 certificate. Also, every three years the latter type of KNX accredited Test Lab is submitted to a
surveillance audit.


-----

In case of staff changes, an accredited KNX Test Lab shall present an updated list of test engineers
working on KNX testing projects, for which the Test Lab is accredited. In case of staff changes since the
accreditation, the KNX accredited Test Lab shall present evidence that any new staff members have either
received internal or external training on the tests, for which the Test Lab had achieved KNX
accreditation, whereby every new KNX test engineer shall at least acquire a KNX certificate for a basic
course and every new KNX test engineer hired for lower layer or security testing shall acquire a KNX
tutor certificate. In the case of staff changes or where the Test Lab does not carry out a minimum of one
test campaign every one and a half years, KNX Association has the right to carry out a surveillance audit
within three months after notification to the accredited KNX Test Lab.

##### 5.8.3 KNX Accreditation of subsidiaries of already accredited KNX Test Labs

In the case where a KNX accredited Test Lab wishes to further accredit own subsidiaries, it can extend
this accreditation under the following conditions.

- The candidate Test Lab is a subsidiary of a KNX Test Lab: both are nationally accredited according
ISO 17025 or if not nationally accredited, accredited according ISO 9001 covering testing operation;

- An appropriate application is submitted to the KNX Association, indicating the address of the
subsidiary and the names of the test engineers concerned;

- An assessment round is carried out by the test engineer of the KNX accredited Test Lab at the
candidate subsidiary Test Lab, of which the audit report is made available to KNX. Prior to the actual
assessment round, an audit plan and the sample test report prepared by the subsidiary Test Lab shall
be made available to and approved by KNX;

- The extension of the accreditation of the subsidiary Test Lab candidate is only possible for tests, for
which the original Test Lab is KNX accredited;

If the above conditions are met and the submitted assessment report is satisfactory assessed by KNX
Association, the subsidiary Test Lab will be entered into the list of KNX accredited Test Labs.

The surveillance of the continuous compliance of subsidiary Test Labs shall be handled in the same way
as is given in clause 5.8.2, i.e. every 3 years, an assessment round shall be carried out. Again, KNX
Association shall be informed about the results by the submission of the corresponding assessment round
report.

Also, staff members at KNX subsidiary test labs shall acquire at least a KNX certificate for a basic course
or for lower layer/security testing a KNX tutor certificate.

##### 5.8.4 Witness Testing by KNX accredited Test Labs
Witness tests by accredited Test Labs are possible under the following conditions.

- The KNX accredited Test Lab carrying out these witness tests delegates a staff member, who is
appropriately qualified.
The qualification of the staff member respectively the accreditation of the Test Lab for this type of
tests (if not yet done) is ensured in two steps.
1. As a first step the responsible staff member of the interested Test Lab is invited as a guest to
participate in a witness test carried out by the association (presupposes the agreement of the
audited member company and the signing of a non-disclosure agreement by the candidate Test
Lab).
2. As a second step, the above staff member carries out the next witness test for the same type of tests,
thereby acting as lead auditor under the supervision of the association. In the case of a positive
evaluation by the association, the above staff member of the Test Lab is considered qualified and
the Test Lab accredited for this type of tests.

- The use of calibrated equipment during the witness tests is ensured and documented in the resulting
test report (e.g. by annexing copies of the calibration certificates of the used equipment).

6.3.4.3.1.1.1 Definitions
Device parameters in MT are implemented in a very specific way:

Structure: ‘ParameterRefRef’  ‘ParameterRef’  ‘Parameter’

- ‘Parameter’ is the actual parameter as it is stored in memory.

- ‘ParameterRef” refers directly to ‘Parameter’, it offers the possibility to override values and
properties.
Example 1: A parameter linked to a particular rocker might need different default values when set up
for switching or dimming.

- ‘ParameterRefRef’ refers directly to ‘ParameterRef’ and is the resource that will be used to visualise
‘Parameter’ in the ETS parameter dialog. ‘ParameterRefRef’ is automatically created when its
associated ‘ParameterRef’ is added to the dynamic structure of the Application Program (see further
down).

- ‘Parameter’ can have more than one associated ‘ParameterRef’, regardless of the target version.

- If the target version is lower than ETS4.0, then ‘ParameterRef” can only have 1 associated
‘ParameterRefRef’.

- If the target version is ETS4.0, then ‘ParameterRef’ can have more than one associated
‘ParameterRefRef’.

The Test Lab will submit the favourable or negative test report to KNX Association with a copy to the
