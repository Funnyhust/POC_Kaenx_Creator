## KNX CERTIFICATION AND LICENCE SYSTEM
# Application Note No 172 rev 0

### Approved by KonCert XX/15 – to be approved in Release for Voting by KTB XX/15 –
 Immediate effect after final voting

**Subject:** Proposal to phase out Vol 9/4/1 and Vol 9/4/2

**Question:**

**Answer:**

It is proposed to phase out the following Volumes

Part 9/4/1 Basic and System Components/Devices – Minimum Requirements – Standardised
solutions - Tests KNX System Conformance Testing BCUs

Part 9/4/2 Basic and System Components/Devices – Minimum Requirements – Standardised
solutions - Tests KNX System Conformance Testing BIMs

of the KNX Standard Version 2.1.

In view of the fact that the Part 9/4/1 and 9/4/2 must be read

- as extensions to other parts of the KNX Specification, i.e

Vol. 3: System Specification
Vol. 4: Hardware Specification
Vol. 6: Profiles
Vol. 8: System Test Specification

- and in conjunction with the specific Product Standard.

- and in conjunction with the EN 50491 series as HBES standard, i.e.

Part 2: Environmental Conditions
Part 3: Electrical Safety
Part 4: Functional Safety
Part 5: EMC

the content is already covered by the documents, named above.

Specific information, e.g. block diagrams of electronis circuits, used electronic components are either no
longer commercially available or superseded.
