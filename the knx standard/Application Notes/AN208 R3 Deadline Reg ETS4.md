## KNX CERTIFICATION AND LICENCE SYSTEM
# Application Note No 208 rev 3

### Approved by KCG in 06/21 – Approved without comments in release for Voting by KTB 07/21 – Immediate effect after approval

**Subject:** Deadline for the submission of products for registration with target version ETS4

**Question:**

**Answer:**

Except for

   - Applications for main certificates for the correction of bugs and minor updates of existing application
programs (to be evaluated and decided on a case-by-case basis by the KNX Certification
Department)

   - Applications for derived certificates

KNX Association will stop accepting new product applications for registration with target version ETS 4 from
end 2023 onwards.

For the above application types given under “except for”, the halting of the registration is scheduled for end
of July 2024.
